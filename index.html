UNC Dataverse

UNC Dataverse is the designated research data repository supported by the Office of the Vice Chancellor for Research at the University of North Carolina at Chapel Hill. UNC Dataverse allows the UNC research community and its partners to archive, catalog, share, and maintain access to data. Open data is freely available to the public, and restricted data requires approval to access.
